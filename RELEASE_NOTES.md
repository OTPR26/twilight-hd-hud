## Items Bank Update

- New TPHD-style Items bank with fixed item positions, a patterned banner,
  darker cells, soft shadows, and Ooccoo's circular bottom-right slot.
- Original Wheel remains available in the mod's **Items Screen** setting.
- **D-Pad Down:** Collection/Save. **Start / +:** Items; press again or B to close.
- Refined Collection/Save and Minimap label alignment.
- Custom Call Midna bindings and controller profiles are preserved. When Midna
  uses Down in Follow mode, Collection/Save uses Right.

### Downloads

- **Desktop-Android:** Windows x64, Linux x64 / Steam Deck, macOS Apple Silicon,
  and Android ARM64.
- **iOS-tvOS:** iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV ARM64.

Download the `.dusk` for your platform, or extract the matching ZIP and use its
included `.dusk`. Keep only one Twilight HD HUD version in your normal `mods`
folder, then fully restart Dusklight. Keep your saves and settings.

Requires **Dusklight v1.4.1-141 or newer**.

The approved preview was tested in-game on macOS. Automated builds cover all six
native targets; this is not a claim of in-game testing on every platform.
