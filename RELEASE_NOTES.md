# Twilight HD HUD v1.3.0 — Fonts & HUD Customization

Choose your text font, fine-tune each part of the HUD, and use clearer Xbox
prompts throughout the mod.

## What's new

- **Four text fonts:** Original, Zen Kaku Gothic New, M PLUS 2, and Dusklight's
  Fira Sans family. The new fonts preserve the game's text spacing and wrapping.
- **Independent HUD sizing:** adjust the controller diamond, D-Pad, and hearts
  from 50% to 125%, in 1% steps, with reset-to-100% buttons.
- **Overall sizing without compounded percentages:** a non-100% overall value
  overrides the individual controls. Return to 100% to restore your saved sizes.
- **BAYX Flipped:** Attack/B on the east, Action/A on the south, Y north, X west.
  This changes HUD presentation, not your controller bindings.
- **Xbox shoulder and trigger prompts:** LB, RB, LT, and RT on both BAYX layouts,
  with Silver and Black Pro artwork.

## Menu and prompt fixes

- HD styling for Options save/discard confirmations and their Yes/No buttons.
- A compact question box that fits its text.
- Correct trigger prompts for Brightness / Screen Ratio and Direct Select.
- Updated Wolf Link Dig artwork and a corrected Bow & Arrow Combo label.
- Simpler settings descriptions; existing saved selections are preserved.

## Updating

Replace the existing mod package, then fully restart Dusklight. Font changes
also require a restart. New fonts cover Western-language message text;
decorative fonts and unsupported characters keep their original artwork.

Use **Overall HUD Size: 100%** to edit individual sizes. To hide the HUD, use
Dusklight's **Minimal HUD** setting.

Requires **Dusklight v1.4.1-141 or newer**. Custom controller bindings remain
unchanged; check that your bindings match your preferred layout.
