## Twilight HD v2.1.0

- Added BOTW-style HUD layouts for ABXY, BAYX, and Universal.
- Added blank silver buttons for Universal, with the previous appearance available as Transparent.
- Clarified the Shoulder & D-Pad Behavior setting and controller-binding guidance.

Requires Dusklight Nightly **v1.4.1-402 or newer**.

The BOTW layouts are visual presets; controller bindings remain configured in Dusklight.
